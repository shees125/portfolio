How I work

My content process

01

Topic discovery & keyword research

I start by identifying content gaps using keyword research tools and SERP analysis. I look at search volume, competition level, and user intent to choose topics that have real ranking potential — not just traffic for the sake of it.

Google Search ConsoleKeyword PlannerSERP Analysis
02

Deep research & source gathering

Before writing a single word, I gather authoritative sources — industry reports, technical documentation, and expert insights. For B2B tech content, accuracy matters as much as readability, so I don't skip this step.

Industry ReportsTechnical DocsCompetitor Blogs
03

Outline & structure planning

A well-structured outline is half the battle. I plan H2/H3 headings, key points per section, and internal linking opportunities before writing — so the draft flows logically and covers all relevant subtopics Google expects to see.

Content OutlineHeading HierarchyInternal Linking
04

Writing & on-page SEO

I write in a clear, direct style — no fluff. Each piece includes optimized meta titles, descriptions, SEO-friendly URLs, proper keyword placement, and readability checks to make sure both readers and search engines are happy.

Meta TitlesSEO-friendly URLsKeyword DensityReadability
05

WordPress publishing & formatting

I handle publishing directly in WordPress — formatting headings, adding featured images, optimizing media alt-text, setting categories and tags, and doing a final pre-publish check before going live.

WordPressMedia OptimizationAlt Text
06

Performance tracking & content updates

After publishing, I monitor performance via Google Analytics and Search Console. I track impressions, CTR, and rankings — then update and improve content regularly to maintain and grow its position over time.

Google AnalyticsSearch ConsoleContent Refresh
